Urban-centre data attribution

Derived from GHS Urban Centre Database 2025 (GHS-UCDB R2024A), version 1.2,
European Commission, Joint Research Centre (JRC), GHSL.
Copyright European Union. Reuse is authorised with acknowledgement of the source.

Dataset: https://data.jrc.ec.europa.eu/dataset/1a338be6-7eaf-480c-9664-3a8ade88cbcd
Source and reuse notice: https://human-settlement.emergency.copernicus.eu/download.php?ds=ucdb

This application extracts city names and 2025 boundaries, rounds coordinates to
three decimal places, and stores a compressed JSON index. The pinned source URL
and checksum are recorded in scripts/build-urban-centres.py and the index.
